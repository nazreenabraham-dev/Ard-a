The Human Behind It
Nazreen
Abraham Stein
Founder & Principal
Permaculture Farmer Ecological Designer Educator Curator Author

A Cowboy. A Pirate. A Samurai.

Nazreen Abraham Stein is a permaculture farmer, ecological designer, and environmental educator — and a retired art curator who spent years curating exhibitions across Malaysia and Berlin.

"Nature is the artist. We are the frame. We design living systems, aesthetically."

His journey from co-founding Minut Init Art Social to living off-grid at an 11-hectare jungle camp in Kemensah, to farming in Bentong, Pahang — then bringing it all together at Mukha Tree TTDI — has shaped a practice where ecology and aesthetics are inseparable.

Nazreen trained in permaculture basics at Tiga Biri, Gopeng, and carries hands-on expertise in soil restoration, syntropic agroforestry, composting, water systems, solar power, and community garden design.

Author of KL Noir Magic (2021). Writer for The Daily Seni, Azucar Magazine, and Juice Online. Co-Art Director of the Belukar Dah Jadi Rimba exhibition at Universiti Malaya (2024).
